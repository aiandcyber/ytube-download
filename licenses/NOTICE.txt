FFmpeg is required.

Android APK: FFmpegKit audio (com.antonkarpenko:ffmpeg-kit-audio:2.2.3, FFmpeg 8.1.2)
  License: LGPL-3.0
  Includes libmp3lame (MP3 encode). Bundled in the APK. Not downloaded after install.

Windows: FFmpeg 9.0.2 essentials, downloaded on first launch from
  https://github.com/GyanD/codexffmpeg/releases/download/9.0.2/ffmpeg-9.0.2-essentials_build.zip
  Extracts ffmpeg.exe, ffplay.exe, and ffprobe.exe.
  License: GPLv3 (Gyan essentials builds). Stored under the app AppData ffmpeg folder.
  SHA-256: 60f467265b1e312373dbcd92200c2618a74850f98d3d078e94296bb3fa2047ba

Android Play: FFmpegKit does not ship the ffplay SDL player. Play uses the system video/audio player.
